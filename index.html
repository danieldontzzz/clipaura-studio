Build a multi-shot vertical montage from video files. Upload sources, set each shot's in/out points, arrange their order and export a 1080 × 1920 MP4.

Choose multiple video files from your iPad. Each source is stored privately in ClipAura. MP4 and MOV recommended; maximum 2 GB per source.

02 — Arrange and trim shots

New uploads start as six-second shots. Adjust start/end seconds and move shots up or down to set the sequence.

Choose or create a project to load its shots.

The overlay is placed at the lower-middle position for the full montage. Stage-one export is silent; music, sound effects and transitions are the next editor upgrade.
